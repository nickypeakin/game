    How to survive the night

    • Goal: stay alive until 6:00 AM (about 7 minutes). If everyone gets caught, you lose.
    • Planks: grab them from the plank piles (hallway & living room) and hold E at a door or window to board it up. Max 3 boards each. You can carry 2 planks.
    • The stalker walks around outside and pounds on doors and windows. Each hit breaks boards. Run there and keep nailing boards on!
    • The minimap (top right) shows how many boards every entrance has. Red = none. Flashing = it's being attacked or it's broken.
    • Flashlight trick: shine your light in his face through a window (one that has fewer than 2 boards) and he'll back off. Boarded-up windows block the light!
    • Power: he can cut the power. Hold E at the fuse box in the hallway to turn it back on. He hits harder in the dark.
    • If he gets in: run, hide in a closet or under a bed (hold E). If he sees you hide, he'll find you. Shining your flashlight on him slows him down. He leaves after a while. Board up the broken entrance after he leaves!
    • Caught? A friend can hold E next to you to help you up.
    • Phone: answer the landline when it rings. The caller might tell you where he's coming next...
    • Batteries are in the kitchen drawer.

    Controls
