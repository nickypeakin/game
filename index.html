    How to play

    • The night: it's 9:00 PM in Apartment 302. Do every chore on the clipboard (on the counter next to the kitchen sink). When they're all done, a sticky note with the safe's code appears on it. The safe in the laundry room has the front door key. Unlock the front door and get out before 6:00 AM.
    • Look at things to use them: aim the dot right at something and press E (or click). Q is a second action, like locking the back door.
    • One thing at a time: you can carry one item (the clipboard, the key, batteries, your dinner...). G puts it back. Your flashlight is in your other hand (F). Pick up batteries and press R to recharge it.
    • Your phone comes out by itself when he texts you (your flashlight goes away while it's out). Press 1 or 2 to text back. Ignore him and he comes looking.
    • Your watch: press T to check the time and when the next curfew is. C reads the clipboard while you hold it.
    • Curfew: the lights go out and you have 20 seconds to get into bed (or on the couch) under the blanket. Look up at the ceiling to shut your eyes. Eyes shut, he can't see you. Eyes open, he drags you out. Out of bed with your flashlight on, and he comes through the window.
    • The thing in the wall climbs out of the hole in the bathroom and hunts you. He runs a little slower than you can sprint. Close doors, hide in a closet (if he sees you get in, he knows), or get in bed and shut your eyes. Loop him around a table too many times and he goes into a rage.
    • Three hits: every hit slows you down. On the third, he drags you away... and you might not wake up.

    Controls

    WASD move  ·  Mouse look (click the game first; Esc frees the mouse)  ·  Shift sprint  ·  E / Click use  ·  Q second action  ·  G put back  ·  F flashlight  ·  R new batteries  ·  T watch  ·  C read clipboard  ·  1 / 2 text back  ·  M map

    Play with headphones. Play with friends: one person hosts, everyone else joins with the code.
