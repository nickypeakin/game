    How to play

    • The story: you're apartment-sitting for your aunt for three nights in Apartment 302 (laid out like Room 302 from Silent Hill 4). Every evening you do your chores, go to bed, and wake up in the middle of the night. Each night is worse than the last. And there's a hole in the bathroom wall that keeps getting bigger.
    • Chores are listed on the left. Some need you to carry things: take a frozen dinner from the fridge to the microwave, eat at the table, carry your plate to the sink, take the trash bag out to the dumpster in the courtyard, get the clothes out of the dryer and put them in your closet, swap a dead light bulb.
    • Doors: every room has a door or an archway. Press E to open or close doors, and Q at the front door to lock it and put the chain on. Lock up before bed.
    • Lights: there's a light switch by every doorway. Lights help you see, but they also help him see you.
    • At night he walks around the building, knocks, looks in the windows, and later cuts the power and tries to get in. Shine your flashlight in his face through a window to scare him off. Fix the power at the fuse box in the laundry room.
    • Planks are on the shelf in the laundry room when you need them. Press E at a window or door to board it up (max 3 each).
    • If he gets in: run, and hide in a closet or under a bed. If he sees you hide, he'll find you. A friend can help you up if you get caught.

    Controls

    WASD move  ·  Mouse look around (click the game first; Esc frees the mouse)  ·  Arrow keys walk and turn  ·  Shift sprint  ·  E / Space / Click use things (just tap)  ·  Q lock / second action  ·  G put down what you're holding  ·  F flashlight

    Play with headphones. Play with friends: one person hosts, everyone else joins with the code.
